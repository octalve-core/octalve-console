# Consolidated commercial follow-up

Paystack refund request is explicitly approved with the existing super-admin authenticator, reserved durably before the supplier POST, and reconciled without retrying uncertain money movement. Independently processed refunds are separate idempotent ledger entries, with a cumulative ceiling and immutable original gross collection. Invoice remains noncollectible; net revenue reporting, refund hold clearance and dispute resolution need separate qualification. Live keys stay in the existing vault. Provider inventory ambiguity holds a request. Supplier reference and fields must be qualified against actual account responses.

References: https://paystack.com/docs/api/refund/ and https://paystack.com/docs/payments/refunds/ . No live requests during CI.
